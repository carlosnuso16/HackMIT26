# CommonGround demo script (~3 minutes)

Reset first: open **My anonymous space** → **Erase local demo data**.

1. **Safety (20s)** — Point at the green privacy banner. Say: fictional data only; alias is the only local save; no eligibility or recruitment.
2. **Community (40s)** — Scroll seed posts. Note topics in the left rail. Switch topic to **Everyday adaptations**.
3. **Local share (40s)** — Compose a short post under Everyday adaptations → **Review before sharing** → read the local-share copy → **Share locally**. Show it appearing at the top of the feed labeled *you · this session*.
4. **Research theme (50s)** — Compose under **Research room** with a sensitive sentence → Review → emphasize “your words will not” → **Add anonymous theme**. Land in Research room; show count bump and “anonymous theme added,” **not** the sentence.
5. **Support + signal (20s)** — **Add local support**. Glance at the right-rail signal (first/second counts can nudge).
6. **Profile (20s)** — Regenerate alias; mention it persists in this browser only. Offer erase again for the next judge.

Close: governance path is visible; a real product would not skip ethics/opt-in.
